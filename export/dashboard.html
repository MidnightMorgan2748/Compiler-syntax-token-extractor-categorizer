Theoretical & Empirical Validation of std::set Logarithmic Bounds

std::set uses a Self-Balancing Red-Black Binary Search Tree. Every insertion verifies uniqueness and rebalances in O(log U) time. In contrast, naive std::vector with linear search exhibits O(N × U) quadratic decay.
